<p>The Edusave Character Award (ECHA) recognises pupils who demonstrate exemplary
character and outstanding personal qualities, and who serve as role models
to their peers.</p>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>Who can be nominated?</strong></h4>
<p>The award is open to all Singapore Citizen pupils across all levels. Nominees
must consistently deomnstrate good character and values particularly in
the following areas:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong><em>Examplary Character and Values</em></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>This includes qualities such as resilience, integrity, solidarity, and
empathy.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For example, pupils who have persevered through challenging circumstances,
overcome adversity, and continued to strive for their best in both academic
and non-academic pursuits.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><em>Civic Resposibility</em></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>This refers to pupils who actively look out for and help those around
them, such as classmates, family members, or neighbours.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Rather than waiting for adult intervention, they take intiative to solve
problems and contribute to making their school and neighbourhood a better,
cleaner, and happier place.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<h4><strong>How to nominate?</strong></h4>
<p>Parents, stakeholders, and staff may submit nominations via our <a href="https://form.gov.sg/6a73585c9407ca4c9e716f2c" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">FormSG link</a> from <strong>7 to 23 August 2026</strong>.
Please note that <u>incomplete or late submissions will not be considered</u>.</p>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>Selection and Endorsement</strong></h4>
<p>Nominess will be shortlisted by a panel of selected teachers and key personnel,
and endorsed by the School Leaders and representatives from the School
Advisory Committee.</p>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>Award</strong></h4>
<p>ECHA Awardees will receive a certificate and an award voucher. The award
quantum are as follows:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Primary 1 to 3: $200</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Primary 4 to 6: $350</p>
</li>
</ul>
